### Project Philosophy
Xivig Text Editor combines the lightning speed and lightweight footprint of **Sublime Text** with the live web previewing and design-centric developer tools of **Phoenix Code**. It was created to provide a distraction-free, keyboard-centric editing experience for developers across Windows, Linux, and macOS.
